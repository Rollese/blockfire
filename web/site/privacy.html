Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 July 2026 · Blockfire is in active development; this policy will be updated as the service evolves.

This policy explains what personal data Blockfire ("we") processes when you play the game or use the player-stats website at stats.blockfire.cc, why, and the choices you have. It is written to reflect what our software actually does.

What we collect

  • Steam identifier (SteamID64): your public Steam ID, used to attribute match results to your profile.
  • In-game statistics: match and event data such as kills, deaths, hits, weapons used, and objective play.
  • Steam display name & avatar: retrieved from Steam only when public profile enrichment is enabled, to label your profile.
  • Technical logs: IP addresses and request metadata recorded by our game servers and web server for operation, security, and abuse prevention.
  • A single session cookie (bf_session) set only if you sign in through Steam, to keep you logged in.

Why we process it (legal basis)

To operate the game service, produce public leaderboards and player profiles, and run anti-cheat / anomaly detection to keep matches fair. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in providing and protecting the service, and — where you sign in — your consent.

How long we keep it

Raw match events are retained for a limited window (by default 90 days) and then pruned; aggregated statistics (totals, leaderboards) are kept for as long as the service runs so your profile remains meaningful. Logs are rotated on a short schedule.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your data. It is processed by a small set of providers acting on our behalf:

  • Valve / Steam — for sign-in (OpenID) and public profile enrichment.
  • Cloudflare — content delivery and protection at the network edge.
  • Our hosting & mail infrastructure — self-managed servers that run the game, website, and email.

Cookies

We use one strictly-necessary session cookie for login, and no advertising or third-party tracking cookies. Because it is essential to a function you request (staying signed in), no consent banner is required.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data — including having your player profile and statistics removed ("right to be forgotten"). To make a request, email privacy@blockfire.cc. We currently handle deletion manually by your player key; please allow a reasonable time to process it. Depending on your country you may also have the right to lodge a complaint with your data-protection authority.
