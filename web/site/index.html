Mission Brief

What is Blockfire?

Blockfire is a low-poly, server-authoritative first-person shooter built for large-scale battles. Up to 128 players clash in Conquest across sprawling maps, tearing through fully destructible buildings with class-based loadouts and tactical, objective-driven combat.

It's in active development. Wishlist now and drop in when the servers go live.
