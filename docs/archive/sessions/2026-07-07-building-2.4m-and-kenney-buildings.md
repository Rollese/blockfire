## Task 1 — Cell height → 2.4 m (owner-chosen GLOBAL CUBE)

- `BuildGrid.CELL_SIZE` 2.0 → 2.4, plus every matching hardcoded-2.0 geometry site: `BuildingKit.CELL`,
  the two preview scripts' `CELL`, `StructureKit._SPEC` widths (wall/sandbag/heavy_barricade), and the
  Python gens (`map_gen.py`, `map_gen_suburb.py`, `showcase_gen.py`).
- **Deleted the `bwall_door` "cell above is the header" hack** — the door is now self-contained in one
  2.4 m cell (fixed ~2.1 m opening + solid header fill). Also rescaled `bwall_window` (its solid fills only
  reached y=2.0, leaving a 0.4 m gap at the top of every window wall at 2.4 m).
- Destruction holes follow the new height automatically (`_promoted_height`/`chunk_hole_xforms` derive from
  `CELL_SIZE`) — verified with `tools/render_destruct_shots.gd`.
- **27 broken absolute-value test assertions** fixed (coordinate math, collapse volume, march distances,
  stair/floor heights, the twostory-climb spawn cell — which shifted from cell 3 to cell 2 at 2.4 m, the
  vault landing — which lands one cell further past the now-deeper blocker). `conquest_suburb` was retuned
  (the deeper industrial bays no longer fit between the south row and the cross-street; hangar dropped from
  the crowded east spur). Regen all three gens — 0 problems.

## Task 2 — Kenney-quality BuildingKit pieces

- **Roofline** (the big win): a parapet + trim coping ring, added by the **renderer** to the TOP wall
  course (`_roof_cap_of` + `_building_bounds.wymax`, mirrored in `building_preview`), continuous around the
  roof **including corners**. Geometry in `BuildingKit._roof_parapet_x/_z`. It's shallow relief on the
  carvable slab — a top wall breaches like any wall and its parapet drops with it (the roofline erodes).
- **Windows** — keystone, projecting stone sill, deeper glazing reveal + mullion. **Doors** — keystone on
  the lintel + a projecting threshold. **Corner quoining** — alternating proud blocks up the outer edge
  (`_corner_quoins`); it reads well even in shadow.

## Collateral fixes (from the owner's live destruct review)

- **Collapse rubble was buried on raised terrain** — `server_main._footprint_ground_cells` stamped
  `brubble` at hardcoded `cell.y=0`; a building on a hill sits at `cell.y>0`, so the rubble landed a cell
  under the terrain (invisible + unwalkable). Now stamped at the building's floor `cell.y`. Real game bug.
- **Rubble read as an artificial lattice** — the per-cell walkable `brubble` pieces (kept — owner chose
  walkable) get a deterministic per-cell spin + scale + in-cell offset + warm/cool tint in
  `world_renderer._build_group_mmis` so it reads as an organic ruin field. Client-cosmetic.
- **QA tooling** — `BF_NOFX` env on `WorldRenderer` suppresses dust/debris/collapse cosmetics so a STATIC
  destruct capture shows unobscured geometry (the frozen FX otherwise read as "floating balls"); `BF_CORNER`
  in `render_destruct_shots.gd` targets a corner piece.

## Deferred — corner destructibility (owner-decided)

`bwall_corner` is an **L of two faces**; `ChunkMask` is a **single-face** 64-bit model, so a carved corner
promoted through `chunk_hole_xforms` collapsed into one flat inward slab. **Interim:** corners are excluded
from client promotion (`_is_promotable`) so they keep a clean L but are **not yet breachable**. Owner chose
the **shared-mask** approach (render+carve both arms from the one mask; a corner breach opens both arms near
the join) as a **focused destruction pass AFTER these buildings**, with its own re-gate. See
`docs/milestones/M11-destructible-buildings.md` + memory `blockfire-corner-destruction-deferred`.
