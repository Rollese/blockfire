## The map & road approach (what exists now)

- **Map:** `maps/conquest_town.json` (the DEFAULT map). Village core of 31 buildings + a road grid + 5 capture points + 2 bases; `world_half=170` (340×340 m). Regenerate with `python3 tools/map_gen.py` — **never hand-edit the JSON/PNGs.**
- **Terrain:** heightmap `maps/heightmaps/conquest_town.png` — **8-bit grayscale, 171×171, 2 m spacing**, decoded by `shared/sim/terrain.gd::build_grid` to a `TerrainGrid`. `Terrain.height_at` is **bilinear**. The **server grounds pawns on this** (so the player's eye-height follows it — "wavy feel" when walking = the eye tracking terrain bumps). Generated by `tools/map_gen.py::gen_town_heightmap`.
- **Roads are PAINTED, not meshes.** A road/sidewalk **surface splatmap** `maps/heightmaps/conquest_town_surface.png` (RGB: **R=asphalt, G=sidewalk**, 1024²) is sampled in the terrain shader (`client/world_renderer.gd::_terrain_material`, uniforms `surface_map/map_origin/map_extent`). Flat road *meshes* are skipped on terrain maps. So a road IS the terrain surface with paint on top → **a road inherits whatever the terrain does underneath it.**
- Generated by `tools/map_gen.py::gen_town_surface_map` (rasterizes the `roads` AABB list) + `add_terrain_to_town` (writes both PNGs + injects the `terrain` block incl. `surface_map`). `shared/sim/map_def.gd` passes `surface_map` through (client-only).

## Why roads look "wavy" — what we found (in order)

1. **Roads are painted on the terrain mesh**, so they pick up ALL terrain variation: the core hills, the 8-bit quantization stairstep, and the shoulder slopes.
2. **8-bit quantization:** height range ~38 m over 256 levels = **0.15 m steps**. A gently-graded road quantizes to tiny terraces; walking, the bilinear eye-height ramps over them → sub-decimeter "bobbing." (We tightened the range dynamically, 0.22→0.15 m, but it's still 8-bit.)
3. **The avenue itself is now DEAD FLAT** — we measured the bilinear eye-height along `x=0`: `total rise 0.00 m, max |2nd-diff| 0.000 m, 1 quantized level`. So the *main road surface* is not the problem anymore.
4. **The sidewalk was painted 2 m BEYOND the leveled road bed**, out onto the sloped shoulder ramp → its outer edge waved with the grass. Fixed in `f5688d7` (flat bed now extends `PAD=2` cells to cover the sidewalk). Owner confirmed "a little better."
5. **CURRENT best diagnosis (last screenshot, unfixed):** the **E-W cross streets extend to x=±152, out into the dramatic countryside perimeter hills, and CLIMB them.** In the shot, roads visibly ramp up green hills in the mid-distance. The *village-core* roads are flat; the *hill-climbing road ends* are the remaining "wavy." **This is the most likely thing to try next but was NOT implemented before the pause.**

### The core tension
Dramatic perimeter hills (owner likes them) + a road grid that covers nearly the whole map = **roads on hills = wavy**. There is very little road-free area for hills to live.
