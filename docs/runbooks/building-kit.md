# Runbook: building templates + the screenshot preview

The destructible map buildings are data-driven prefabs in `buildings/*.json` (a list of pieces, each
`{type, offset:[x,y,z] in 2m cells, yaw}`), rendered by the procedural `client/art/building_kit.gd`.
The server stamps a map's `buildings[]` at boot. Cells are 2 m; walls are 2-3 cells tall (4-6 m);
doors clear a standing pawn (~2.3 m opening); windows are ~1 m square set high; roofs are flat with a
realistic eave overhang.

## The 16 building templates (as of 2026-06-19)

Residential: `shed` (3×3, tiny), `cottage` (3×4), `family_a` (4×4), `family_b` (5×4), `house` (5×5,
4 m walls), `townhouse` (3×5, 3-storey-tall narrow), `villa` (6×7, many windows), `lhouse` (L-shaped),
`test_twostory` (walkable 2-story — interior stair, **upper floor still 2 m ceiling**, see below).
Commercial/industrial: `bunker` (4×4 squat), `tower` (3×3 tall, walled), `office` (5×5, 3-cell),
`supermarket` (10×7 storefront), `warehouse` (8×6, bay door), `factory` (8×7, 3-cell), `hangar`
(11×7, very wide).

Generated by `tools/build_gen.py` (a generic footprint builder — rectangles + arbitrary cell sets for
L-shapes). Regenerate/extend there.

## Iterating the LOOK without a playtest (screenshot preview)

`client/art/preview/building_preview.tscn` renders one building from 4 angles (iso/front/side/eye) and
saves PNGs. It needs a real renderer (GPU + display), so run it on the desktop, not headless.

The dev loop (from game2, helper at `/tmp/shot.sh <name>`): sync the building files to the desktop,
render there, pull `/tmp/pv_<name>_{iso,front,side,eye}.png`, then view the images. **Render host:** the desktop (`192.168.1.194`, RX 9070 XT) OR the laptop (`192.168.1.128`, thinkpad, integrated GPU) — both Wayland (`wayland-0`). The laptop is the reliable fallback when the desktop is asleep (`/tmp/shotlap.sh <name>` targets it; `/tmp/shot.sh` targets the desktop). Render buildings
**sequentially** — parallel godot instances fight over the desktop GPU/window and produce blank frames.
Always check multiple angles; a single iso view hides door/window height and interior detail.

## Showcase / playtest

`maps/conquest_showcase.json` places all 16 templates on a 4×4 grid (world_half 120, central objective)
for touring the set. The combat arena `maps/conquest_arena_buildings.json` carries a curated subset on
the outer ring (away from the central bot fight, so they survive long enough to inspect).

## Known limits / follow-ups

- **Walkable 2-story ceiling is 2 m** per floor (single-flight stair). A real ~4 m floor needs
  multi-flight (switchback) stairs — deferred.
- **Flat roofs only.** Pitched/gabled roofs are hard on the per-cell grid (a roof spans a whole
  building, not a cell) — the eave overhang is the stand-in until a sloped-roof mesh is added.
- **2.6 m EU-standard ceilings** aren't grid-aligned (cells are 2 m → 2 m or 4 m). Exact 2.6 m would
  need a `BuildGrid.CELL_SIZE` change (touches collision/chunks/many tests) — not done.
