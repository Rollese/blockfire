Evidence `docker/srvlog-20260615-211516.log`. **Design changes ratified during the gate** (`combat-depth.md`): *finishing* removed — DOWNED pawns are **immune to weapon damage** (bleed-out or revive only); bleed retuned (`BLEED_RATE` 2→1, `BLEEDOUT_FLOOR` −50→−240 ≈ 8 s); `REVIVE_RANGE` 2.0→3.0 m; revive intent **latched** server-side (survives packet loss); **friendlies always replicated** (interest culls enemies only) so downed squadmates are revivable at fleet density.

> **P3 — Movement: PASS (2026-06-16).** 128-bot fleet (plan 12 tasks; Tasks 6/9 two-stage reviewed): `winner=1 elapsed=317s cap_events=2 peak tick=23.46ms (<33.3)`; `climbs=9 vaults=16` (both ≥1), agg 18.7 Mbit/s, `dropblk=5`. Evidence `docker/srvlog-20260616-115725.log`; ≤48 smoke also PASS (`climbs=4 vaults=7 peak=15.78ms`). 245 unit tests green. **Impl:** ladder climb + auto-vault + platform floor as pure `Ladder`/`Vault` helpers driven by `SimLoop`; drop-shoot prevention as a pure `Combat.drop_shoot_blocked` predicate off `last_stance_change_tick`; `climbing` in the free state-byte bit 7 (`vaulting` deferred to M7); `MapDef` parses `ladders`/`platforms`/`prebuilt`. A deterministic **bot movement-drill** (~1 in 8 bots cycle a behind-base ladder/sandbag station, off the objective lanes) guarantees `climbs≥1`/`vaults≥1` every match.

## Non-scope (design decisions)
No sliding · no parachutes · no swimming/water · no self-revive (self-bandage only halts bleed) · no gadget unlock gating. Vehicle repair kit is defined here (Engineer gadget B) but implemented in M5; RPG anti-vehicle damage lands in M5 (structure + pawn damage active from M4.5).
